# EventPilot
### AI-Powered Event Operations Assistant

EventPilot is an event operations automation system built with n8n, AI, and Google Sheets.

It helps event teams organize research and manage opportunities involving speakers, sponsors, and vendors.

## The Problem

Event teams may spend significant time researching potential speakers, sponsors, and vendors, organizing findings, and preparing reports.

EventPilot is designed to streamline these repetitive tasks.

## Key Features

- Speaker research and pipeline management
- Sponsor research and opportunity tracking
- Vendor research and organization
- Google Sheets integration
- On-demand pipeline reporting
- Structured AI outputs for organized records

## How It Works

1. A user requests an event-related research task.
2. An AI agent processes the request and organizes the results.
3. The workflow routes records to the appropriate pipeline.
4. Google Sheets stores the organized information.
5. Users can request reports based on stored pipeline data.

## Technology Stack

- n8n — Workflow automation
- AI language models — Research and information processing
- Google Sheets — Data storage and pipeline management

## Project Status

Portfolio project demonstrating AI-assisted research, workflow automation, and business operations.

## Important Note

Research results should be reviewed and verified before business decisions or outreach. The system must not invent contact details or unsupported claims.
